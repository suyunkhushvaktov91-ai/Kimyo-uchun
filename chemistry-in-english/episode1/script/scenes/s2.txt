Take one hair from your head. It looks very thin, right? It is only about one tenth of a millimetre wide.

Now imagine that you cut something into smaller and smaller pieces. Again and again. Finally, you reach pieces so small that you cannot see them, even with a normal microscope.

Here they are. About one million of them could stand side by side across the width of that one hair. One million!

Each tiny piece is called an atom. An atom is a very small particle, and all matter is made of atoms. Your phone, the water, the air, and you.

The word "atom" comes from the old Greek word "atomos". It means "cannot be cut". For a long time, people believed that atoms were the smallest things in the world.

Today we know more. We can look inside an atom. So let's do it.
