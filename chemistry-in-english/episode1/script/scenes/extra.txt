Bonus time: the pronunciation corner. Some of today's words sound different in English and in Uzbek. Listen and repeat after me.

Nucleus. NEW-klee-us. In Uzbek we say "yadro". In English: nucleus.

Molecule. MOL-uh-kyool. The stress is at the beginning: MOL-ecule. Not mo-le-ku-LA.

Electron. ih-LEK-tron. The stress is on the second part: e-LEC-tron.

Neutron. NEW-tron. Proton. PROH-ton.

Atom. AT-um. The stress is on the first part: AT-om.

Element. EL-uh-ment. Again, the stress is on the first part: EL-ement.

Matter. MAT-er. Mass. Charge. Shell. Short words. Say them clearly.

Great! Practise these words every day, and soon you will sound like a real scientist.
