Let's go back to the electrons. They do not fly around the nucleus randomly. They move in layers. We call these layers shells.

In our simple model, the first shell can hold up to two electrons. The second shell can hold up to eight.

Let's build some atoms. Hydrogen has one electron. It goes into the first shell.

Helium has two electrons. Now the first shell is full.

Carbon has six electrons. Two in the first shell, and four in the second shell. We write this as two, four.

Oxygen has eight electrons. Two, and six.

Neon has ten electrons. Two, and eight. Now both shells are full.

Atoms with full shells are very stable. They do not like to react with other atoms. That is why neon is so calm.

Did you notice something? In every atom we built, the number of electrons is the same as the number of protons.

Carbon has six protons and six electrons. Six plus and six minus. They balance each other. So the whole atom has no charge.
