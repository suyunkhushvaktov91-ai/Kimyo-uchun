Atoms are not usually alone. Many atoms like to join together. When two or more atoms join together, they make a molecule.

Look at this one. Two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom. Together they make a molecule of water. We write it as H two O.

One small glass of water has more water molecules than there are grains of sand on all the beaches of the Earth.
