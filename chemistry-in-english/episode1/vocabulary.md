# Episode 1 — Vocabulary (12 words)

## Example sentences

1. **Matter** — *Water, air and stone are all matter.* — Suv, havo va tosh — bularning hammasi modda.
2. **Atom** — *A human hair is about one million atoms wide.* — Inson sochining qalinligi taxminan bir million atomga teng.
3. **Nucleus** — *The nucleus is in the centre of the atom.* — Yadro atomning markazida joylashgan.
4. **Mass** — *Almost all the mass of an atom is in its nucleus.* — Atom massasining deyarli hammasi yadroda.
5. **Proton** — *A carbon atom has six protons.* — Uglerod atomida oltita proton bor.
6. **Neutron** — *A neutron has no charge.* — Neytronning zaryadi yo'q.
7. **Electron** — *Electrons move around the nucleus.* — Elektronlar yadro atrofida harakatlanadi.
8. **Charge** — *A proton has a positive charge, and an electron has a negative charge.* — Proton musbat, elektron esa manfiy zaryadga ega.
9. **Atomic number** — *The atomic number of oxygen is eight.* — Kislorodning tartib raqami sakkiz.
10. **Element** — *Scientists know 118 elements.* — Olimlar 118 ta elementni bilishadi.
11. **Shell** — *The first shell can hold up to two electrons.* — Birinchi qavatda ko'pi bilan ikkita elektron bo'ladi.
12. **Molecule** — *A water molecule is made of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.* — Suv molekulasi ikkita vodorod va bitta kislorod atomidan tashkil topgan.

## Pronunciation traps for Uzbek speakers

- **molecule** — stress on the first syllable: **MOL**-ecule (not mo-le-ku-**LA**).
- **nucleus** — two "ee"-like sounds at the end: NEW-klee-**us**, not "nukleus".
- **electron**, **proton**, **neutron** — the last syllable is short and weak in British English: -tron /trɒn/, never "-tro-on".
- **element** — stress on the first syllable: **EL**-ement.
- **charge** — starts with "ch" /tʃ/ as in *chair*, not "sh" or "z" (Uzbek *zaryad*).
