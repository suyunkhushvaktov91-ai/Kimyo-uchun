# Episode 1 — Quiz

12 questions. Questions 1–5 are the **on-screen quiz** for the video (one card per question, about 6 seconds each, after cue `[6.3]`). The full quiz can go in the description, a pinned comment, or a worksheet.

---

## Part A — Choose the correct answer

**1.** What is in the centre of an atom?
- a) an electron
- b) the nucleus
- c) a shell

**2.** Which particle has a **negative** charge?
- a) proton
- b) neutron
- c) electron

**3.** The **atomic number** is the number of …
- a) protons in the nucleus
- b) neutrons in the nucleus
- c) atoms in a molecule

**4.** An atom has 8 protons. Which element is it?
- a) carbon
- b) oxygen
- c) neon

**5.** A water molecule (H₂O) has …
- a) one hydrogen atom and two oxygen atoms
- b) two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom
- c) two hydrogen atoms and two oxygen atoms

**6.** How many electrons can the **first shell** hold (in our simple model)?
- a) 2
- b) 6
- c) 8

---

## Part B — True or false?

**7.** A neutron has a positive charge.

**8.** Most of an atom is empty space.

**9.** A normal atom has the same number of protons and electrons.

---

## Part C — Fill in the gap

Word bank: **matter · element · mass**

**10.** __________ is anything that has mass and takes up space.

**11.** An __________ is a pure substance made of only one kind of atom.

**12.** Almost all the __________ of an atom is in its nucleus.

## Answers

| # | Answer | Why |
|---|--------|-----|
| 1 | **b** — the nucleus | The nucleus is the small, heavy centre of the atom. |
| 2 | **c** — electron | Protons are positive, neutrons have no charge, electrons are negative. |
| 3 | **a** — protons in the nucleus | Carbon has 6 protons, so its atomic number is 6. |
| 4 | **b** — oxygen | Oxygen has atomic number 8. Carbon is 6, neon is 10. |
| 5 | **b** — two hydrogen + one oxygen | H₂O: "H two" = two hydrogen atoms, "O" = one oxygen atom. |
| 6 | **a** — 2 | The first shell holds up to 2, the second up to 8. |
| 7 | **False** | A neutron has no charge. It is neutral. |
| 8 | **True** | If an atom were a stadium, the nucleus would be a pea in the middle. |
| 9 | **True** | Plus and minus balance, so the whole atom has no charge. |
